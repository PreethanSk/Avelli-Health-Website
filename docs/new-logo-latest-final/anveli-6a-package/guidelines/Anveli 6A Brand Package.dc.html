anveli — logo system

Four circles, one point. Every layer of a life (years, providers, records) is a different size, and all of them touch the same point: the person. The mark is a symbol plus a Geist wordmark, on ink with an ice-blue core.

03 · Construction

All four circles are internally tangent at a single point on the vertical axis, so every centre sits at y = tangent − r. The three rings share one stroke of 0.075R. The core is solid.

At 32px and below, use the optical version: one ring at 0.19 of the box, plus the core. The middle rings would otherwise close up into a blot.

06 · Living mark
Idle · breatheInner rings swell ±7% out of phase, staying tangent the whole time. 7s period. Use for the app splash and resting states.
Syncing · accrueA new ring is born from the core and every layer moves out one step, while the oldest fades past the edge. It loops seamlessly. Use for importing and syncing.
Record added · pulseThe core swells and the pulse travels outward ring by ring, 80ms apart, over 0.9s. It fires once, then returns to idle.

10 · Don't
Don't centre the rings. That's a target, not a person.
